Phase 6 – Scrubland: 6 small trees (BP_Tree1, BP_Tree2, BP_Tree4, BP_Tree6) scaled 0.4-0.7× scattered between town and mesas (Y = 5000-8000, varied X).

Phase 7 – Final: take_screenshot() from a cinematic angle showing full world (camera ~[15000,-8000,4000] looking NW), then check_collisions() and fix any overlaps. Take one final screenshot.
